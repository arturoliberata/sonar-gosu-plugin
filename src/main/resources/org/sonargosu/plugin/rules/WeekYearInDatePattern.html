In date patterns, y is the calendar year and Y is the week-based year, meant to be used with week numbers (w). They differ in the last days of December and the first days of January: with "YYYY-MM-dd", December 31, 2025 is printed as 2026-12-31, because that day belongs to week 1 of 2026. Policies, claims and invoices dated around New Year get wrong dates.

This rule checks the patterns given to new SimpleDateFormat(...), DateTimeFormatter.ofPattern(...) and applyPattern(...).

Noncompliant code example

var format = new SimpleDateFormat("YYYY-MM-dd")

Compliant solution

var format = new SimpleDateFormat("yyyy-MM-dd")
var weekFormat = new SimpleDateFormat("YYYY-'W'ww")   // week-based year with week numbers is fine
